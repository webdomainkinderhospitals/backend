India being the second most populous country with a large number of Infants born annually, pregnancy is considered as a condition which demands only clinical approach and not as a time to celebrate and cherish moments. Whether you're a first-time mom-to-be or are pregnant with your second child (or third or more!), those nine months leading up to birth are an undeniably wondrous and life-changing experience, that needs to be celebrated. Gone are the days of overly staid restrictions imposed by family and society and unsolicited advices for women during pregnancy period. Today, expecting mothers’ see pregnancy as a time to rejoice, shoot pregnancy moments, prepare baby’s journal, create family tree and not to hide behind layers to cover up the baby bump and deal with substantial distress reinforcing a false conviction of pregnancy.
Birth is the epicenter of women’s power. We at the Kinder Hospitals relish each and every moment of “Pregnancy happiness” of expecting mothers.
